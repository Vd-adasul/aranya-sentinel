# 20. FINAL PRODUCT VISION

The finished application should demonstrate a complete forest-protection intelligence workflow.

An authority opens the platform.

The command centre displays monitored forest regions.

Satellite intelligence identifies a suspicious forest-cover change.

The system generates a geographic disturbance polygon.

The incident is assigned an investigation priority.

An analyst opens the satellite comparison.

Drone imagery is added.

Grounding DINO identifies potentially relevant vehicles or equipment.

SAM 2 segments the detected objects.

The platform links the relevant observations when the geographic evidence supports the relationship.

The analyst reviews the collected evidence.

The incident is verified or rejected.

An evidence-backed investigation report is generated.

The system maintains a traceable record of the entire process.

The natural-language assistant can answer questions about the actual incident records.

The final product should communicate that this is not just computer vision, but an integrated AI-assisted forest monitoring and investigation platform.

**Build for actual operational functionality, excellent geospatial visualisation, trustworthy AI inference and professional product quality.**

Begin with Phase 0 and execute the project systematically.
